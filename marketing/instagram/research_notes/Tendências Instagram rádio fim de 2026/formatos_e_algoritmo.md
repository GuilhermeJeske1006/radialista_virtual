# Instagram feed formats, content mechanics and algorithm signals (late Nov 2026), for B2B in Brazil

Scope note: researched on 25 Nov 2026. I label anything from 2025 or earlier as older. Many 2026 figures come from vendor or agency blogs rather than Meta. I flag those where they matter. I could not open Mosseri's own Instagram or Threads posts, so his statements here come from press or blog reports.

## 1. Which static and carousel formats and meme templates are trending in Brazil (Oct-Nov 2026), and which stay relevant into December?

### Takeaway
Most named viral trends in Nov 2026 are Reels built on audio. The ones that work as static posts or carousels are few: "It's NO-vember" (ends 1 Dec), year-end recaps, and evergreen carousel formats. Those evergreen formats are didactic, save-worthy "solve one problem" carousels, before/after cards, split-screen "news on top, analysis below", POV, recurring series with a fixed look, and deliberately "messy" carousels made of screenshots, memes and annotations. I found no reliable Portuguese-language source listing Brazilian meme templates specific to Nov 2026.

### Cited Findings
**Named trends in Nov 2026 (global and US-led; weekly tracker updated 20 Nov 2026)**
- New Engen's tracker lists 10 November trends. It describes only two as working as a static image or carousel:
  - **"It's NO-vember"**: stacked "no" lines naming habits to drop. It works as a single image or carousel and suits service businesses and coaches. The tracker states: "The pun expires on December 1."
  - **"Turkey Charcuterie Boards"**: a photo carousel tied to Thanksgiving. It is US-only and fades after Thanksgiving. — [New Engen, Instagram Trends Nov 2026](https://newengen.com/insights/instagram-trends/)
- The other November trends on the same tracker are Reels only:
  - "I Feel Like I Haven't Done Much This Year", a month-by-month recap set to Harry Styles' "Coming Up Roses"
  - "Dear Santa, I'm a Size", set to Wham!'s "Last Christmas" and used by salons, clinics, car dealers and boutiques
  - "Christmas Wrapping Paper Reveal", an announcement revealed by tearing gift wrap
  - "Add to Cart", tied to pre-Black Friday shopping
  - "No, No, No... Yes!", where the boring parts are labelled "no" and the payoff "yes"
  - "Lately on My SD Card", "My Moon My Man Soft Launch" and "Paid Me No Mind"
  - The tracker notes that audio-driven trends usually peak within 7-10 days, while holiday-classic audio "often runs longer". — [New Engen](https://newengen.com/insights/instagram-trends/)
- **"Remember November 2026 Is Coming"**: an AI-generated video of a polar bear singing, set to an Oliver Tree song. It emerged around Sept 2026 and spread across TikTok and Reels as covers, edits and calendar jokes. One Instagram variant reached more than 92k likes in three days. — [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/remember-november-2026-is-coming); [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/articles/remember-november-2026-meme-viral-193000262.html)
- **Other memes from the week of 19-20 Nov 2026**: Arc'teryx-jacket memes, a Roblox basketball clip ("Huss Huss Huss") used for lip-syncs, an office pizza party with "one slice per plate", and two rams butting heads as "firstborn daughter and dad". — [NapoleonCat, trending memes Nov 2026](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/trending-memes/); [Memestand, 20 Nov 2026](https://memestand.com/top-memes-today/)
- **Counterpoint on carousels**: Lightreel's weekly report for 14-21 Nov 2026 found no carousel with high-confidence reach that week and says Reels dominate trends. — [Lightreel](https://lightreel.ai/blogs/whats-trending-on-instagram)

**Carousel templates trending in 2026** (I saw these only through search summaries, not full pages)
- **"Kinda Chic"**: each slide uses a different colour with one light piece of advice.
- **"Nobody saw me"**: a carousel satirising hustle culture, where "none of those sacrifices actually happened". The page's dates are confusing. — [SocialBee, latest Instagram trends (19 Nov 2026)](https://socialbee.com/blog/latest-instagram-trends/)
- **"Messy" carousels**: deliberately unpolished, made of screenshots, memes and hand-written annotations. Listed among 2026's attention-grabbing formats. — [whaaat.ai, carousel trends 2026](https://www.whaaat.ai/blog/instagram-carousel-trends-2026-12-formats-blowing-up-right-now)

**Brazilian trend guide (Rafael Terra, a Meta Creator Partner, published 10 Jan 2026)**
- Of his 26 trends for 2026, these are usable as static posts or carousels:
  - #1 solving one specific problem, because usefulness drives saves and shares
  - #2 split screen, with the news or situation on top and your analysis below
  - #5 quick before/after "transformation cards"
  - #6 repost-worthy "that's me" content
  - #10 analysis of niche data
  - #15 POV, recommended for silent pains and dilemmas
  - #19 a recognisable recurring format with the same look and opening
  - #20 current vs. future situation, without miracle promises
  - #22 linking outside culture to the niche (his example: what the BBB reality show teaches about competing for attention)
  - #26 "TV programming": the feed treated as a schedule with fixed weekly segments
- His suggested carousel structure is: what it is, why it matters, how to apply it, common mistakes, final checklist.
- He also recommends 1-2 quotable cards in the middle and closing cards like "Checklist final" or "Copie e cole isso". — [Rafael Terra](https://rafaelterra.com.br/tendencias-conteudo-instagram-2026/)
- He also lists **comment-a-keyword → DM automation** that sends the full guide (trend #18). — [Rafael Terra](https://rafaelterra.com.br/tendencias-conteudo-instagram-2026/)

**Brazilian memes and catchphrases in 2026**
- BBB 26 (Jan-Apr 2026) produced many catchphrases, such as "quinta série" (Ana Paula) and "panquequinha" (Gabriela). Press described many of them as already part of everyday vocabulary. These are older than Oct-Nov 2026. — [NSC Total](https://www.nsctotal.com.br/entretenimento/quinta-serie-panquequinha-e-mais-os-bordoes-que-marcaram-o-bbb-26); [Estado de Minas, Apr 2026](https://www.em.com.br/cultura/2026/04/7402993-bbb-26-relembre-os-memes-que-marcaram-a-edicao.html)
- In May 2026 Cariúcha turned her catchphrase "bonita pra caramba" (viral since 2009) into a cosmetics brand. — [O Tempo, 19 May 2026](https://www.otempo.com.br/opiniao/fernando-berenguel/2026/5/19/cariucha-revela-que-criou-uma-marca-com-meme-de-profissao-reporter-faco-dinheiro)

### Inferences
**For December 2026**
- **"It's NO-vember"** is the only named trend that is both static-friendly and current. It only fits if published on or before 30 Nov. Example: "It's NO-vember: não pra madrugada no piloto automático, não pra locutor faltando no feriado, não pra vinheta repetida."
- **The year-end recap ("I feel like I haven't done much this year")** can run through December as a carousel, one slide per month. It suits a "Locufy em 2026" or "sua rádio em 2026" story.
- **The Dear Santa / Christmas framing** carries into December. A static version would read: "Querido Papai Noel, eu quero um locutor que não tire folga no Natal."

**Evergreen formats that fit radio owners and managers**
- These are mostly not meme-dependent, which suits an audience that is likely older and less meme-native:
  - myth vs. fact ("IA na rádio: mito ou verdade")
  - checklists (a "plantão de fim de ano" checklist, a "grade de Natal e Réveillon" checklist)
  - "expectativa vs realidade" (holiday shifts, overnight shows)
  - POV ("POV: é 24 de dezembro, 23h, e o locutor da noite avisou que não vem")
  - "me diga que você trabalha em rádio sem me dizer"
  - this-or-that (for example "vinheta x locução ao vivo", "AM x FM")
  - split-screen news + analysis
- **"Grade de programação"** (programming schedule) is native vocabulary for this audience. Building the feed as fixed weekly segments (trend #26) doubles as on-brand storytelling.

**Fit problems for this audience**
- Trends that depend on US culture (Thanksgiving, Arc'teryx, Roblox) or on licensed music do not fit static B2B posts aimed at Brazilian radio.
- The AI polar bear meme is date-bound (it expires with November). It also leans on AI-generated imagery, which may be unwise given the new AI labels (see section 2).

### Gaps
- I found no Portuguese-language source (B9, Meio & Mensagem, Propmark, mLabs, Etus) cataloguing Brazilian Instagram meme templates or slang for Oct-Nov 2026. A Meio & Mensagem search returned nothing relevant.
- I could not verify whether "Kinda Chic" or "Nobody saw me" carousels have traction in Brazil.
- I could not confirm a Brazilian equivalent of the "Remember November" meme.

## 2. What official Instagram and Meta changes in 2025-2026 matter for feed posts?

### Takeaway
The core ranking guidance is unchanged since early 2025. Mosseri names watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as top signals, with sends weighted more for reaching non-followers. The 2025-2026 changes that matter most for a static or carousel brand are these:
- the 3:4 profile grid (Jan 2025)
- the 5-hashtag cap (Dec 2025)
- carousels you can reorder after posting (Mar 2026)
- a stop to recommending photos and carousels from aggregator accounts (Apr 2026)
- "Your Algorithm" controls reaching the main feed (Jun 2026)
- music you can change on any post (Jul 2026)
- an "AI Info" label on all posts (Jul 2026)

### Cited Findings
**Ranking signals (older, 2025, still cited as current in 2026)**
- Mosseri said to focus on average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach.
- He called sends per reach one of the most important ranking signals. Likes weigh slightly more for followers, sends slightly more for non-followers.
- Instagram does not publish exact weights, and the "3-5x" multipliers in some blogs are not from Mosseri. — [Buffer, algorithm guide 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/); [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers); [Social Media Today, 2025 (older)](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/)
- Sources disagree on when the sends-per-reach remarks were made: some say mid-2024, others early 2025. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/instagram-algorithm-shift-why-sends-matter-more-than-ever/521389/); [Uppbeat, "carousels and sends"](https://uppbeat.io/blog/creator-news/whats-working-on-instagram-right-now-carousels-and-sends-says-adam-mosseri)
- **Carousel re-show mechanic (older, Oct 2024)**: Mosseri reportedly said Instagram often shows the next slide to people who scrolled past a carousel. This gives carousels a second chance in feed. — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)
- **Stories reshares (Apr 2026)**: Mosseri reportedly said that sharing your own feed post to your Story does not help its reach. This is a secondary blog report. — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)
- **Views as the main metric (since 2025)**: "views" replaced impressions as the primary metric across Reels, photos, carousels and Stories. — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)
- **Insights (older, Aug 2025)**: Instagram added a "Repost" metric for posts and Reels, plus Reels "Retention" and "Skip Rate". — [SocialBee updates tracker](https://socialbee.com/blog/instagram-updates/)
- **Unverified claims**: a September 2026 list of "four ranking principles" and an October 2026 Mosseri comment on sends both circulate in blogs with no primary source. — [Search summary of 2026 algorithm blogs, e.g. Creator Lane](https://creatorlanehq.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-what-changed)

**Grid and aspect ratio (older, Jan 2025)**
- In January 2025 Instagram moved the profile grid from 1:1 squares to 3:4 rectangles, rolling out over about two months. Mosseri's reason: most uploads are now vertical.
- The visible grid tile for a standard post is 1012 x 1350 px. A 1080 x 1350 (4:5) upload is therefore cropped slightly at the sides in the grid. — [Inquirer Technology](https://technology.inquirer.net/?p=140070); [Oktopost](https://www.oktopost.com/blog/?p=17190)
- Brazilian 2026 format guides still recommend 4:5 (1080 x 1350) as the preferred feed size, with 1080 x 1080 still accepted. — [Envox](https://envox.com.br/criacao-de-posts/novos-formatos-do-instagram-2026-guia-tamanhos-dicas/agencia-de-marketing-digital/trafego-pago/vendas/)
- In Sept 2025 Edits was said to be possibly adding 3:4 export for carousels. — [SocialBee tracker](https://socialbee.com/blog/instagram-updates/)

**Grid order**
- Jun 2025: "Edit Profile Grid" added for creators.
- May 2026: grid reordering widely tested.
- Jun 2026: all users can reorder posts on their profile grid. — [SocialBee tracker](https://socialbee.com/blog/instagram-updates/)

**Hashtags**
- Social Media Today (18 Dec 2025) reported a cap of five hashtags per caption on posts and Reels, rolling out gradually.
  - Instagram's wording: "using fewer (up to 5) more targeted hashtags, rather than many generic ones".
  - An earlier test had limited many users to three.
  - Mosseri had said hashtags "don't work" to increase reach but help "let people know what a post is about". — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/); [Business Today, 19 Dec 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19)
- Later says the cap counts the caption and comments together. — [Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-hashtags/amp)
- Conflict: SocialBee's tracker logs "Nov 2025: posts and Reels limited to 3 hashtags", which reflects the earlier test. Social Media Today's later report says five is the universal limit. — [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/instagram-updates/) vs [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)

**Carousel product changes in 2026**
- 23 Mar 2026: you can rearrange slides in a published carousel. You still cannot add media after posting. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-allows-users-to-rearrange-carousel-posts/815490/)
- Mar 2026: you can edit thumbnails on posted content. — [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/instagram-updates/)
- 11 May 2026: Instagram confirmed it is testing captions on each slide of a carousel. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-tests-captions-on-each-post-within-carousels/819917/)
- Older, Jul 2025: Instagram tested like counts for individual carousel frames. — [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/instagram-updates/)
- From 7 Oct 2026: a carousel editor in the Edits app, iOS first. This comes from a secondary source only. — [Gradezilla](https://gradezilla.org/instagram-carousel-edits-app/)
- Nov 2026: Instagram "could" raise the carousel limit from 20 to 30 items. This is unconfirmed; the current limit is 20. — [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/instagram-updates/)

**Music on posts**
- May 2026: a "Change audio" button.
- Jul 2026: users can change the music on any post. — [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/instagram-updates/)

**Originality**
- 30 Apr 2026: Instagram stopped recommending photo and carousel posts from aggregator accounts in Explore and other recommendation surfaces. Distribution to followers is unchanged. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-updates-algorithm-to-benefit-original-creators/819016/)

**Trial Reels (Reels only; there is no trial option for feed photos or carousels)**
- Older, Jun 2025: global rollout.
- Apr 2026: Instagram limits how many trial Reels you can post within 30 days.
- Nov 2026: the Edits desktop app, launching "next month", includes Trial Reels. — [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/instagram-updates/)

**"Your Algorithm" controls**
- Dec 2025: launched in the US.
- Jan 2026: rolled out for Reels to English-speaking users globally.
- Apr 2026: expanded to Explore.
- Jun 2026: expanded to posts in the main feed. — [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/instagram-updates/)

**AI labels and authenticity**
- Mar 2026: Instagram began working on a label for profiles that mainly post AI content.
- May 2026: an "AI Creator" account label.
- Jul 2026: an "AI Info" label on all posts.
- Sep 2026: an "AI-generated profile" label for accounts featuring AI-generated people. — [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/instagram-updates/)
- Mosseri's year-end memo (31 Dec 2025, older but framing 2026):
  - "Authenticity is becoming infinitely reproducible."
  - Raw, unpolished content will be favoured.
  - Judgment of content will shift from how it looks to who made it and why.
  - Labelling and verification of authentic media are needed, though detection will get harder.
  - Commentators dispute whether a "raw aesthetic" proves anything. — [Om Malik](https://om.co/2026/01/01/what-is-instagrams-adam-mosseri-really-saying-in-his-year-end-memo/); [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/social-media/articles/instagram-says-ai-killed-curated-155610900.html)

**Reposts**
- Older, Jul-Aug 2025: a Repost feature rolled out.
- Jun 2026: you can remove other people's reposts of your posts. — [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/instagram-updates/)

### Inferences
- **Design for sends.** For a static or carousel B2B account, the practical goal is "sends per reach": posts radio people forward to a colleague or a WhatsApp or Direct group. Saves come second. Likes matter mostly for existing followers.
- **Safe area.** Keep headline text and logos inside the central about 1012 px of a 1080 x 1350 canvas, so the 3:4 grid crop does not cut them. The first slide also acts as the grid tile, which matters for the profile's "programação" look.
- **Hashtags.** Use at most 5 specific hashtags (for example #radio #radiobrasileira #locutor #gestaoderadio #inteligenciaartificial). Rely on keyword-rich captions and on-image text for discovery, not on hashtags.
- **Originals only.** Repurposed screenshots of others' content without added value risk the aggregator downgrade. Original visuals and data are safer.
- **AI labels.** An AI-radio brand that uses AI-generated people or images may get "AI Info" labels. Being upfront ("voz gerada por IA") fits Mosseri's authenticity framing. Real screenshots of the product panel and real radio studios add credibility.
- **Fixing posted carousels.** Post-publish reordering and thumbnail editing make it cheap to correct a carousel's cover slide after seeing early performance.

### Gaps
- I could not access about.instagram.com, creators.instagram.com or Mosseri's original posts directly. All Mosseri quotes are via press or blogs.
- I found no official 2026 Meta statement that changes the ranking-signal list. The September and October 2026 "new principles" claims are unverified.
- I found no official information on carousel music changes specific to 2026 (photo carousels with music already existed before 2025).
- I found nothing official on Brazil-specific product changes.

## 3. What do 2026 benchmarks say about carousels vs single images vs Reels, especially for B2B?

### Takeaway
Across 2026 datasets, carousels lead the feed formats on engagement and saves. Reels lead on raw reach. Single images are declining on both. The size of the carousel lead depends on whether engagement is divided by followers or by reach. No 2026 dataset I found breaks this out for B2B or SaaS on Instagram.

### Cited Findings
**Socialinsider (by followers)**
- Methodology: engagement is (likes + comments) ÷ followers. Organic posts only, from business accounts with 1K-1M followers. About 67.5M posts and about 579k accounts. Page published 20 Feb 2026, updated 4 Nov 2026.
- Its "2026" benchmarks are actually 2025 values. Its sample figures are internally inconsistent: 35M vs 67.5M posts, and a "24% YoY" drop vs 0.50→0.48%.
- No B2B or industry breakdown is given. — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)

| Format | 2025 | Q3 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Carousels | 0.55% | 0.50% |
| Reels | 0.52% | 0.48% |
| Images | 0.37% | 0.35% (up from 0.33%) |
| All formats | 0.48% | 0.45% |

- Posting mix in 2025: brands posted about 8 Reels, 5 carousels and 7 images per month. — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)

**Buffer (by reach; report published 5 Mar 2026)**
- Median engagement as a share of reach: carousels 6.90%, single images 4.44%, Reels 3.31%.
- A separate analysis of 4M+ posts found:
  - Reels reach 1.36x as many people as carousels and 2.25x as many as single images.
  - Carousels get 2.09x the engagement rate of Reels and 1.55x that of single images.
- That separate analysis covers Jan 2022 to Oct 2024, so it is older data.
- Typical Instagram median engagement fell from about 7.3% (2024) to about 5.46% (2025).
- Replying to comments was linked to about 21% more engagement.
- No B2B breakdown is given. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/)

**Metricool Instagram Study 2026 (24.3M posts, as cited by mLabs)**
- Carousels get 9x more saves than single images.
- Single images fell -21.96% in reach and -25.41% in interactions in one year.
- Reels get more than 4x the interactions of a single image.
- 70% of a post's views happen in the first 72 hours. — [mLabs, Estatísticas do Instagram 2026](https://www.mlabs.com.br/blog/estatisticas-do-instagram/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-trends/)

**Outlier: Ordinal (by impressions, non-click engagement)**
- Feed images 3.85%, carousels 3.19%, Reels 2.73%. I saw this only via a search summary.
- Another reported figure: carousels about 109% ahead of Reels on engagement per person reached, vs about 12% in 2024.
- Buffer's own write-ups frame the carousel-vs-Reels gap inconsistently. — [Futuretweets](https://futuretweets.com/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-algorithm-2026/); [Bereel](https://bereel.pro/blog/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-for-brands)
- On Socialinsider data, Reels still win reach for accounts under 50k followers. Carousels are best for saves, and Reels lead on comments and shares. — [Bereel](https://bereel.pro/blog/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-for-brands)

**Brazil**
- HypeAuditor + Metricool (Propmark, 3 Sep 2026): 3.5M Brazilian creator accounts and 83M posts, from Jun 2025 to Jun 2026.
  - Carousels make up 14-27% of posts.
  - Carousels can "superar os reels em alcance e curtidas", especially above 1M followers, and are comparable or better across audience sizes.
  - About 90% of Brazilian profiles post once a week or less and lose about 2% of followers per year.
  - Posting 2-3 times a week was linked to 26% growth, and more than 10 posts a week to 93%.
  - The audience is 43% aged 25-34 and 61% women.
  - This is creator data, not brand or B2B data. — [Propmark](https://propmark.com.br/digital/reels-ou-carrossel-qual-gera-mais-engajamento-no-instagram-em-2026/)
- Opinion Box 2026 (Brazil):
  - 47% of users concentrate their access between 18h and midnight.
  - 77% save content and 84% share it.
  - 76% use Direct, and 31% talk to brands there.
  - 42% clearly identify AI-made posts, and 16% see no difference.
  - DataReportal 2026: Instagram reaches 147M people in Brazil through ads. — [mLabs](https://www.mlabs.com.br/blog/estatisticas-do-instagram/)
- mLabs on Brazilian retail (Oct-Dec 2025, older): Reels are retail's main showcase. Amazon (7.9 posts/week) and Carrefour (5.9) favour reach through content with fewer posts. — [Nosso Meio](https://nossomeio.com.br/amazon-lidera-alcance-e-magalu-domina-engajamento-no-instagram-do-varejo-brasileiro-aponta-mlabs/)
- Older, mLabs 2023: carousels had the best feed engagement (7.2%, vs 5.8% for photos and 6.3% for video). Carousels were the best option for companies with up to 500k followers. — [mLabs, via search summary](https://www.mlabs.com.br/blog/post-carrossel-instagram/)

**B2B-specific claims**
- MarTech recommends educational carousels for B2B: methodology explainers, research, case walkthroughs and FAQs. Its reasoning is that saves and shares signal buying interest. — [MarTech](https://martech.org/b2b-marketing-on-instagram-what-you-need-to-know/)
- One unverified claim: B2B posts published within 12 hours of each other saw about 17% lower engagement than posts spaced more than 24 hours apart. — [Futuretweets](https://futuretweets.com/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-algorithm-2026/)

### Inferences
- **Make the carousel the default feed unit.** For a small, young B2B account like Locufy, the 1080 x 1350 carousel should be the default for education and saves, with single images used sparingly for announcements or a quote card. Reels or Trial Reels should carry discovery, given Reels' reach lead under 50k followers.
- **Posting times.** Given Opinion Box's 18h-midnight finding, evenings are a reasonable default for Brazil. B2B decision-makers may differ, so check in-app Insights.
- **Cadence.** Brazilian accounts that post 2-3 times a week grow while those posting weekly or less shrink. That makes a sustainable cadence more important than hitting trends.
- **Engagement health.** The fact that 70% of views land in the first 72 hours, and that replies lift engagement about 21%, both argue for being active in comments right after posting.

### Gaps
- I found no 2026 Instagram benchmark broken out for B2B, SaaS, software or media and broadcasting accounts, globally or in Brazil.
- I found no 2026 mLabs or Etus brand-format report for Brazil. The mLabs Índice, a monthly per-format index, exists but its figures were not retrievable.
- I could not access Metricool's primary 2026 study page with methodology.

## 4. Which formats do Brazilian B2B and SaaS brands use successfully?

### Takeaway
I could not find documented, sourced 2026 case studies of the Instagram feed strategies of RD Station, Conta Azul, Nubank PJ, Hotmart or radio-industry suppliers. Brazilian agency guidance converges on didactic carousels, recurring branded series, keyword → DM automation and "pain" or "that's me" content.

### Cited Findings
- My searches for RD Station's 2026 Instagram carousel strategy returned only agency guides and a carousel-ad case tagged with an RD Station partner agency. Nothing described RD Station's own approach. — [Search results, e.g. Postig guide](https://postig.com.br/en/blog/guia-completo-carrosseis-instagram-2026); [Agência Gram](https://agenciagram.com.br/blog/carrossel-instagram-como-fazer-2026)
- Rafael Terra's 2026 guide covers the trends his clients use (his list includes Intelbras, Sicredi, Bradesco and STIHL), but it gives no brand-specific case examples. Relevant B2B-style tactics in the guide:
  - problem-solving content
  - niche data analysis
  - real cases with context: starting point, strategy, difficulties, decisions
  - a fixed editorial grid
  - keyword → DM content delivery — [Rafael Terra](https://rafaelterra.com.br/tendencias-conteudo-instagram-2026/)
- Brazilian consumers do interact with companies on Instagram:

### Inferences
- **Brand-safe adaptations for radio broadcasters** (my own suggestions, not sourced trends):
  - "Me diga que você trabalha em rádio sem me dizer" (cue sheets, "tá no ar?", holiday shifts)
  - "Expectativa: feriado de Natal tranquilo / Realidade: grade inteira para cobrir"
  - "Opinião impopular: a madrugada da sua rádio merece locutor"
  - "Isso ou aquilo: vinheta gravada ou locução ao vivo?"
  - "Mito ou verdade: IA na rádio soa robótica?"
  - "Não, não, não... sim!", applied to the pains of a small station's staff
- **Avoid:**
  - BBB or reality-show catchphrases, which are stale and can carry fan-war connotations
  - political memes, including ones from the 2026 Brazilian election year
  - date-bound memes past their date
  - licensed-music audio trends, because static posts and business accounts often cannot use them
  - AI-generated people imagery, because of labels and the trust issues Mosseri describes
- **Fit with the audience.** Radio owners and managers are likely older and more conservative in tone than meme-native audiences. Light humour built on radio's own insider jokes (routine, the studio, the overnight shift, holidays) is likely to outperform imported internet slang, and it is more "sendable" to colleagues.
